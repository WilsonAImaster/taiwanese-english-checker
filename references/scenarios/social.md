# 社群與個人品牌情境：LinkedIn、IG、YouTube

適用：LinkedIn貼文與個人簡介、IG英文說明文字、YouTube英文標題與描述。

等級：🔴 錯誤｜🟠 誤會｜🟡 不自然

---

### S1. Hello everyone, I am… Today I want to share… 🟡
- ❌ Hello everyone, I am Wilson. Today I want to share some tips about AI.
- ✅ I used AI to cut my weekly report time from 3 hours to 20 minutes. Here's how:
- **為什麼**：社群貼文的第一行決定別人會不會點開。用最有吸引力的結果或數字開場，自我介紹放後面或省略。

### S2. 用逗號串很多句子 🔴
- ❌ We went to the trade show in Las Vegas, it was amazing, we met many new clients.
- ✅ We went to the trade show in Las Vegas. It was amazing, and we met many new clients.
- **為什麼**：中文可以用逗號一路串下去，英文兩個完整的句子不能只用逗號連接（comma splice），要用句號或連接詞。

### S3. Welcome to follow me. 🟡
- ❌ Welcome to follow me for more AI tips!
- ✅ Follow me for more AI tips!
- **為什麼**：中文「歡迎追蹤」直翻。英文直接說Follow me for…，或Hit follow if you want more…。

### S4. recommend you to… 🔴
- ❌ I recommend you to try this tool.
- ✅ I recommend trying this tool. ／ I recommend that you try this tool.
- **為什麼**：recommend後面不能接「人＋to＋動詞」，要接-ing或that子句。

### S5. 專業貼文裡用hahaha和!!! 🟡
- ❌ So happy to get this award!!! hahaha
- ✅ I'm honored to receive this award, and grateful to the team that made it possible.
- **為什麼**：LinkedIn是工作場合，連續驚嘆號和hahaha會降低專業感。IG可以輕鬆一點，但也要看你的品牌定位。
