# 學術與升學情境：留學讀書計畫、論文、投稿

適用：留學申請的讀書計畫（SOP）與個人陳述（Personal Statement）、學術論文與摘要、投稿信。

等級：🔴 錯誤｜🟠 誤會（可能被審查者扣分）｜🟡 不自然

---

### A1. With the development of… 🟡
- ❌ With the development of technology, AI has become more and more important.
- ✅ Hospitals in Taiwan now use AI to read 40% of chest X-rays, yet few studies have examined…
- **為什麼**：With the development of…是台灣學生最常見的開場，審查者看到就知道是範本句。直接從具體的問題或數據開始。

### A2. more and more 🟡
- ❌ More and more people use online learning.
- ✅ An increasing number of people use online learning. ／ Online learning has become increasingly common.
- **為什麼**：more and more偏口語，在學術寫作裡用太多會顯得稚嫩。

### A3. Since I was young, I have been interested in… 🟡
- ❌ Since I was young, I have been interested in computers.
- ✅ When our family shop lost half its customers to online stores in 2019, I started teaching myself how e-commerce sites work.
- **為什麼**：讀書計畫的「從小就對…有興趣」是審查者看最多的開頭。用一個具體的經驗開場，才會被記住。

### A4. researches 🔴
- ❌ Many researches show that…
- ✅ Many studies show that… ／ Research shows that…
- **為什麼**：research是不可數名詞，不加s；要算數量就用studies。

### A5. According to the research… 沒有出處 🟠
- ❌ According to the research, students learn better with videos.
- ✅ Mayer (2009) found that students learn better from words and pictures than from words alone.
- **為什麼**：學術寫作每個主張都要有來源。「根據研究」卻沒說哪個研究，審查者會直接質疑。

### A6. It is well known that… 🟡
- ❌ It is well known that social media causes anxiety.
- ✅ Several studies have linked heavy social media use to anxiety (e.g., …).
- **為什麼**：「眾所皆知」在學術寫作裡是偷懶的說法，而且常常其實沒那麼確定。

### A7. such as… etc. 🔴
- ❌ such as Japan, Korea, Taiwan, etc.
- ✅ such as Japan, Korea, and Taiwan
- **為什麼**：such as已經表示「舉例、沒有全部列出」，後面再加etc.是重複。

### A8. This paper wants to… 🟡
- ❌ This paper wants to find out…
- ✅ This paper aims to examine… ／ This study investigates…
- **為什麼**：論文不會「想要」，學術寫作用aim、seek、investigate。

### A9. e.g.和i.e.混用 🔴
- ❌ Some Asian countries, i.e., Japan and Korea, …
- ✅ Some Asian countries, e.g., Japan and Korea, … ／ the capital of Japan, i.e., Tokyo
- **為什麼**：e.g.是「例如」（只舉部分例子），i.e.是「也就是」（說的是全部）。用錯會讓意思完全不同。
