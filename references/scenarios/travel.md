# 旅遊與生活情境

適用：訂房與詢問Email、餐廳點餐、問路、客訴、出差時的日常對話。

等級：🔴 錯誤｜🟠 誤會（可能顯得不禮貌）｜🟡 不自然

---

### T1. Where is the toilet? 🟡
- ❌ Where is the toilet?
- ✅ Where's the restroom?（美國）／ Where are the toilets?（英國）
- **為什麼**：在美國，toilet通常指馬桶本身，直接問toilet聽起來有點太直白；美國說restroom或bathroom。

### T2. Give me a coffee. 🟠
- ❌ Give me a coffee.
- ✅ Could I get a coffee, please? ／ I'll have a coffee, please.
- **為什麼**：中文「給我一杯咖啡」很正常，英文的Give me…聽起來像命令，店員會覺得你沒禮貌。

### T3. Your service is very bad. 🟠
- ❌ Your service is very bad. I want a refund.
- ✅ Our room wasn't cleaned yesterday, and the AC isn't working. Could you help us with this?
- **為什麼**：直接批評對方只會讓人防衛。具體說出發生什麼事，再提出你希望怎麼處理，比較容易得到補償。

### T4. luggages 🔴
- ❌ Where can I put my luggages?
- ✅ Where can I put my luggage? ／ Where can I put my bags?
- **為什麼**：luggage和baggage都是不可數名詞，不加s；要算件數就說bags或pieces of luggage。

### T5. Is here the bus stop? 🔴
- ❌ Is here the bus stop?
- ✅ Is this the bus stop for the airport? ／ Is the bus stop near here?
- **為什麼**：中文「這裡是公車站嗎」直翻。英文here是副詞，不能當主詞，要用this或換個句型。

### T6. Can you take a photo for me? 🟡
- ❌ Can you take a photo for me?
- ✅ Could you take a photo of us?
- **為什麼**：take a photo for me比較像「幫我代拍」，對方可能以為你要他拍別的東西。請人幫你拍，說of me／of us比較清楚。

### T7. I'm not used to eat spicy food. 🔴
- ❌ I'm not used to eat spicy food.
- ✅ I'm not used to eating spicy food.
- **為什麼**：be used to的to是介系詞，後面要接-ing，跟look forward to一樣。
