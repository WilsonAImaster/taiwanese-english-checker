# 30條台式英文地雷

這裡收的不是初學者的錯，而是**英文不錯的人也常寫、寫的人以為是對的**台式英文。

等級：🔴 錯誤｜🟠 誤會（可能冒犯或被誤解）｜🟡 不自然

---

## 一、語氣與禮貌

### 1. Please kindly…／Kindly be informed… 🟡
- ❌ Please kindly send me the price list.
- ✅ Could you please send me the price list?
- **為什麼**：please和kindly都是「請」，疊在一起像過度客氣，母語者讀起來僵硬，有時甚至像在諷刺。Kindly be informed也是同一類的老派說法。

### 2. I want to… 🟠
- ❌ I want to ask for a quotation.
- ✅ I'd like to ask for a quotation.
- **為什麼**：中文說「我想要」很正常，英文的I want在請求時聽起來像在下命令。I'd like…或Could you…比較禮貌。

### 3. You must… 🟠
- ❌ You must pay before shipment.
- ✅ Payment is required before shipment. ／ Please make sure payment is made before shipment.
- **為什麼**：must直接對人用，語氣像主管命令下屬。把重點放在規定或流程上，對方比較能接受。

### 4. ASAP 🟠
- ❌ Please reply ASAP.
- ✅ Could you reply by Friday, October 17? We need to confirm the shipping date.
- **為什麼**：ASAP沒有說明期限，又帶有催促感。給一個具體日期加上原因，對方更容易配合。

### 5. Hope you can understand. 🟠
- ❌ The price will increase 5%. Hope you can understand.
- ✅ The price will increase by 5%. Thank you for your understanding.
- **為什麼**：Hope you can understand聽起來像「你最好要能體諒」，帶有一點勉強對方的意思。

### 6. Do you understand? 🟠
- ❌ (會議中) Do you understand?
- ✅ Does that make sense? ／ Please let me know if anything is unclear.
- **為什麼**：直接問對方「你懂嗎？」像在質疑對方的理解能力，聽起來居高臨下。

### 7. 過度道歉：Sorry for my poor English. 🟡
- ❌ Sorry for my poor English. Sorry to bother you again.
- ✅ Thank you for your patience.
- **為什麼**：商務場合一直道歉會削弱你的專業感。把sorry換成thank you，語氣立刻變得有自信。

### 8. Our price is cheap. 🟠
- ❌ Our price is cheap and the quality is good.
- ✅ Our prices are competitive for this quality.
- **為什麼**：中文說「便宜」是優點，英文的cheap常帶有「廉價、品質差」的意思。報價要說competitive或affordable。

## 二、中文直翻

### 9. 多加介系詞：discuss about／contact with／attend to 🔴
- ❌ Let's discuss about the price. I will contact with you. I will attend to the meeting.
- ✅ Let's discuss the price. I will contact you. I will attend the meeting.
- **為什麼**：中文說「討論『關於』」「『跟』你聯絡」，但這些英文動詞後面直接接受詞。

### 10. 少了介系詞：reply me／explain me 🔴
- ❌ Please reply me. Can you explain me the process?
- ✅ Please reply to me. Can you explain the process to me?
- **為什麼**：reply和explain後面接人時要加to。

### 11. The price is too expensive. 🔴
- ❌ The price is too expensive.
- ✅ The price is too high. ／ This product is too expensive.
- **為什麼**：「價格」只有高或低；說「東西」才用expensive。

### 12. Welcome to contact us. 🟡
- ❌ Welcome to contact us if you have any questions.
- ✅ Feel free to contact us if you have any questions.
- **為什麼**：中文「歡迎與我們聯絡」直翻。英文的Welcome to…後面接地方（Welcome to Taipei）；要說「歡迎你做某事」用Feel free to…或You're welcome to…。

### 13. Wish you have a nice day. 🔴
- ❌ Wish you have a nice weekend.
- ✅ Have a nice weekend! ／ I hope you have a nice weekend.
- **為什麼**：中文「祝你有個美好的週末」直翻。英文的wish後面接子句是「但願（不可能的事）」，祝福要用hope，或直接說Have a nice…。

### 14. How about your opinion? 🟡
- ❌ How about your opinion?
- ✅ What do you think? ／ What's your view on this?
- **為什麼**：How about…是用來提議（How about Friday?），不是用來問看法。問意見直接說What do you think?。

### 15. I have a question want to ask you. 🔴
- ❌ I have a question want to ask you.
- ✅ I have a question for you. ／ There's something I'd like to ask you.
- **為什麼**：中文「我有一個問題想問你」可以把兩個動詞串在一起，英文一個子句只能有一個主要動詞。

### 16. There are many people like… 🔴
- ❌ There are many customers like this model.
- ✅ Many customers like this model. ／ There are many customers who like this model.
- **為什麼**：中文「有很多人喜歡」直翻。英文There are…後面要接who／that才能再放動詞，或乾脆去掉There are。

### 17. Notice me 🔴
- ❌ Please notice me when the goods arrive.
- ✅ Please let me know when the goods arrive. ／ Please notify me when the goods arrive.
- **為什麼**：notice是「注意到」，不是「通知」。通知要用notify或let me know。

## 三、文法常錯

### 18. 不可數名詞加s：feedbacks／staffs／equipments 🔴
- ❌ Thanks for your feedbacks. Our staffs will contact you.
- ✅ Thanks for your feedback. Our staff will contact you. ／ One of our staff members will contact you.
- **為什麼**：feedback、equipment、information、advice是不可數名詞；staff是「全體員工」的集合名詞，指人數要說staff members。

### 19. everyday和every day 🔴
- ❌ I check my email everyday.
- ✅ I check my email every day.
- **為什麼**：分開寫的every day是「每天」；連在一起的everyday是形容詞「日常的」（everyday life）。

### 20. 現在完成式＋過去時間 🔴
- ❌ We have sent the samples yesterday.
- ✅ We sent the samples yesterday. ／ We have already sent the samples.
- **為什麼**：中文的「已經…了」常被翻成have done，但只要句子裡有yesterday、last week這種明確的過去時間，英文就要用過去式。

### 21. 結尾句：Look forward to hear from you／Waiting your reply 🔴
- ❌ Look forward to hear from you. ／ Waiting your reply.
- ✅ I look forward to hearing from you.
- **為什麼**：look forward to的to是介系詞，後面要接-ing；Waiting your reply少了for，單獨一句也像在催人。這兩句幾乎每封台式英文信都會出現。

### 22. I think it's not… 🟡
- ❌ I think it's not a good idea.
- ✅ I don't think it's a good idea.
- **為什麼**：中文把否定放在後面（我覺得不好），英文習慣把否定放在think前面，語氣也比較委婉。

### 23. Here is Wilson. 🟡
- ❌ Hi, here is Wilson from ABC Company.
- ✅ Hi, this is Wilson from ABC Company.
- **為什麼**：中文「這裡是Wilson」直翻。電話和Email自我介紹用This is…或My name is…。

## 四、商務Email習慣

### 24. 稱呼：Dear Sir／Mr. + 名字 🟠
- ❌ Dear Sir,（已知對方叫Thomas Weber）／ Dear Mr. Thomas,
- ✅ Dear Mr. Weber, ／ Hi Thomas,
- **為什麼**：Mr.／Ms.後面接「姓」，不是名字；Mr. Thomas會讓對方覺得你搞不清楚他的名字。知道名字還寫Dear Sir，則顯得你沒做功課。

### 25. 什麼都用confirm 🟡
- ❌ I want to confirm with you about the delivery time.
- ✅ Could you let me know the delivery time? ／ I'd like to check the delivery time with you.
- **為什麼**：confirm是「確認一件已經說過的事」。如果是問新資訊，用ask、check或let me know比較精準。

### 26. Please be noted that…／as below 🔴
- ❌ Please be noted that the price has changed. Please see the details as below.
- ✅ Please note that the price has changed. Please see the details below.
- **為什麼**：be noted是「被注意到」，主詞是事情不是人；請對方注意要說Please note。as below也是亞洲商務信的習慣，英文直接說below就好。

### 27. Please revert. 🟠
- ❌ Please revert to me by Monday.
- ✅ Please get back to me by Monday.
- **為什麼**：revert當「回覆」是南亞、東南亞的商務用法；美國和歐洲的讀者理解為「恢復原狀」，可能看不懂。

### 28. We are a professional manufacturer. 🟡
- ❌ We are a professional manufacturer with good quality.
- ✅ We have made LED lighting for 15 years and export to 20 countries.
- **為什麼**：professional、good quality人人都會說，外國客戶讀了沒有感覺。換成具體的數字和事實才有說服力。（例句的數字請換成你自己的）

### 29. 日期寫成03/04 🟠
- ❌ The goods will ship on 03/04.
- ✅ The goods will ship on March 4.（或4 March）
- **為什麼**：美國讀成3月4日，英國、歐洲、澳洲多半讀成4月3日。月份用英文寫出來，就不會差一個月。

### 30. 金額只寫$ 🟠
- ❌ The price is $500.
- ✅ The price is USD 500. ／ The price is US$500.
- **為什麼**：$可能是美元、新台幣、加幣或澳幣，很多國家的貨幣都用$。報價一定要寫幣別。
