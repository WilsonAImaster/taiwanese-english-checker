# 範例：參展後的LinkedIn貼文

> 這篇貼文的每一句，都是很多人發LinkedIn時常寫、自己以為是對的句子。人名是虛構的。
> 結果是2026-11-03用本skill（Claude）實際執行後的輸出，經作者校對。不同AI、不同次執行，措辭會略有不同。

## 輸入

```text
Hello everyone, I am Amy. Today I want to share my experience about attending the trade show in Las Vegas.
We went to the show last week, it was amazing, we met many new clients.
I have learned a lot of things and I recommend you to visit it next year.
So happy to see our booth full of people!!! hahaha
Welcome to follow me for more sharing about trade shows.
```

使用者說明：這是我的LinkedIn貼文

## 輸出

情境：社群與個人品牌

找到7個問題：2個文法錯誤、5個讀起來不自然。

| # | 原句 | 等級 | 建議改法 | 為什麼 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello everyone, I am Amy. Today I want to share… | 🟡 | Our booth at the Las Vegas trade show was packed last week, and we met many new clients. | 第一行決定別人會不會點開，用最吸引人的結果開場，自我介紹可以省略（社群S1） |
| 2 | We went to the show last week, it was amazing, we met many new clients. | 🔴 | It was an amazing week. | 英文兩個完整的句子不能只用逗號連接；這句的資訊已經搬到第一行，剩下的感想獨立成一句（社群S2） |
| 3 | I have learned a lot of things | 🟡 | I learned a lot, especially about [one thing you learned] | a lot of things太空泛，讀者想知道的是「學到什麼」，點出一件事才有分享價值（規則外） |
| 4 | I recommend you to visit it | 🔴 | I recommend visiting | recommend後面不能接「人＋to＋動詞」，要接-ing或that子句（社群S4） |
| 5 | So happy…!!! hahaha | 🟡 | I'm so happy to see so many people stop by our booth! | LinkedIn是工作場合，連續驚嘆號和hahaha會降低專業感（社群S5） |
| 6 | Welcome to follow me | 🟡 | Follow me | 中文「歡迎追蹤」直翻，英文直接說Follow me for…（社群S3） |
| 7 | more sharing about trade shows | 🟡 | more trade show tips | sharing當名詞用在這裡很像中文「更多分享」直翻，母語者會說tips、insights或stories（規則外） |

**改寫後全文**

方括號裡請換成你自己的真實內容。

```text
Our booth at the Las Vegas trade show was packed last week, and we met many new clients.
It was an amazing week. I learned a lot, especially about [one thing you learned], and I recommend visiting next year.
I'm so happy to see so many people stop by our booth!
Follow me for more trade show tips.
```

**最值得記住的一點**：英文貼文的第一行就是標題，把「結果」放最前面，Hello everyone和自我介紹都可以拿掉。
